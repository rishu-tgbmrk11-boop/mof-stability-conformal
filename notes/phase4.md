## 1. Price of recall

**Definition.** The price of a target recall r is the fraction of ALL MOFs that must be kept so that a fraction r of the truly stable MOFs is kept. Here it is computed with the threshold known exactly: t = the floor((1-r) n_pos)-th smallest positive score, kept = share of all MOFs with score >= t, on the pooled group-aware calibration + test MOFs. This isolates the discrimination of the scores from the noise of one calibration draw (the conformal single-split numbers are in `notes/phase2.md` and `notes/phase3.md`). Intervals are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals over **groups** (1,000 resamples).

**Synthetic scores.** For each stage the real labels and real group ids of the pooled MOFs are kept; scores are drawn from a binormal model (negatives N(0,1), positives N(d,1), d = sqrt(2) Phi^-1(AUC target)) so the AUC is controlled from 0.55 to 0.95, with scores independent of group (no within-group dependence; an optimistic simplification). One dataset per AUC value (seed fixed), so the sample AUC differs from the target by sampling noise (listed below, and used as the x-position in the figure). The analytic curve is the infinite-sample binormal price, kept = pi r + (1-pi)(1 - Phi(d + Phi^-1(1-r))). **These are simulated, not measured**, and real score distributions need not be binormal.

**Reading.**
- Recall is expensive when discrimination is modest. For the analytic binormal curve at the thermal prevalence (0.52), a target recall of 0.95 still keeps 0.89 at AUC 0.70, 0.82 at AUC 0.80, 0.70 at AUC 0.90, 0.62 at AUC 0.95 of all MOFs; reaching a kept fraction below 0.5 at recall 0.95 would need an AUC far above any stage model here.
- The binormal curve is only a rough guide to the real stage models: at their own AUC the real kept fractions deviate from it by up to 0.034 (solvent Phase 2 at recall 0.95: 0.952 vs 0.918), about the size of the intervals, so the data neither support nor contradict the binormal fit (not tested formally); read 'what an AUC improvement would buy' as an approximation.
- The stage AUCs (about 0.64-0.78 here) imply keeping roughly 0.73-0.87 of MOFs at recall 0.90 for the Phase 3 models; the solvent and water stages filter little at high recall.
- Synthetic points use one simulated dataset per AUC with the real sample size; their intervals reflect sampling and group structure only, and sample AUCs can differ noticeably from the target on the small water set (see brackets).

## 2. Limitations

| limitation | what it is | size / evidence | effect on the claims |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group proxy, not MOFid | groups are connected components over refcode family and DOI (DOIs exist only for solvent-table MOFs); no MOFid in the released data | 2,777 groups for 4,963 MOFs; largest group 35 | Duplicates or near-duplicates in different groups would leak across splits and make group-aware metrics and conformal coverage optimistic. The independent review measured part of it: 42 of 589 thermal, 10 of 425 solvent and 7 of 232 water test MOFs have a training MOF in another group within 0.5 z-scored feature distance, with nearest-neighbour label agreement 0.81 / 1.00 / 0.71 against 0.67 / 0.64 / 0.66 for all test MOFs (`notes/review.md` M1) |
| Only 92 MOFs with all labels, 17 positives | the cascade can be scored out-of-sample only on MOFs present in all three tables | 92 MOFs, 17 stable at all three stages | cascade recall intervals are wide (Clopper-Pearson about [0.71, 1.00] at 16-17 of 17); consistent with recall >= 1 - alpha, cannot confirm it, and the guarantee is not proved for this population (`notes/phase2.md` section 4); the count 17 depends on the stability cut-offs (`notes/phase5.md` section 4); the 5 repetitions are not independent |
| Configuration-selection leak in the cascade | Phase 3 configurations were chosen by CV on the training groups of the single split and are reused in every cascade fold | some of those training MOFs lie in cascade held-out folds | small, configuration-level optimism in the Phase 3 cascade numbers; Phase 2 cascade (released hyperparameters) has no such selection |
| 60/20/20 split | one group-aware split (seed 0): 2,977 / 993 / 993 MOFs; models see 60% of MOFs (the released scripts use 80%) | test sets 589 / 425 / 232 MOFs (thermal / solvent / water), 139-313 stable | wide intervals; a different split gives different numbers; all models are somewhat weaker than with 80% training |
| PAC (training-conditional) variant unverified | Beta-based threshold with delta = 0.1 | partitions reaching the target: 77.6%-91.8% across cells vs nominal >= 90% | do not claim a high-probability guarantee; only the marginal guarantee (mean recall >= 1 - alpha) matches the data; the check cannot reach 90% even for a valid rule (iid simulation: 82.7-83.9%, `notes/review.md` M4) |
| Marginal guarantee, exchangeability assumed | the split conformal guarantee holds on average over random assignments of groups to calibration/test, if calibration and deployment MOFs are exchangeable | about half of single calibration draws fall below target; under leave-clusters-out shift the mean recall fell short (`notes/phase5.md` section 3) | a single deployed threshold can miss its target, and a shift to new families can lower recall |
| Released labels, no external test set | all numbers use the released tables | | recall is relative to the released labels |
