# Related work

Search date 2026-10-23, web search only. **Verification status:** for every entry below I saw the title, URL and a search-engine summary or abstract; I did **not** open or read any full text. Details that came only from a search snippet are marked "(snippet)". Author lists or venues I could not see are marked "not verified". Nothing here was checked against the papers' theorems or numbers. Please open the links before citing.

Our setting, for the 'how it differs' lines: per-stage and cascade split-conformal **recall control** (threshold = k-th smallest calibration-positive score) for three MOF stability filters (thermal, solvent, water), using **group-aware** (proxy-group) calibration and test splits, a union bound across the stacked stages, and a measured 'price of recall' (kept fraction vs AUC). Data: released optimof tables, 4,963 MOFs.

## (a) Conformal prediction / conformal risk control, in general and for materials

| citation | link | what it does | how ours differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Angelopoulos, Bates, Fisch, Lei, Schuster, "Conformal Risk Control", ICLR 2024 (arXiv 2208.02814) | https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/f3549ef9b5ff520a7e41ff3cc306ab2b-Abstract-Conference.html | Extends split conformal prediction to bound the expected value of a monotone loss, e.g. a false-negative rate (snippet). | Our per-stage rule is the false-negative-rate (recall) special case; we add a cascade over three stages with a union bound, group-aware calibration, and an empirical validity check on small materials data. We do not claim anything beyond the marginal guarantee. |
| Bates, Angelopoulos, Lei, Malik, Jordan, "Distribution-Free, Risk-Controlling Prediction Sets" (arXiv 2101.02703; journal venue not verified) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02703 | Wraps a black-box predictor so expected loss is below a chosen level with high probability, using a holdout set and concentration inequalities (snippet). | Closest to our 'PAC' variant; our Beta-threshold version did not reach its nominal 90% in our check (`notes/phase3.md`), so we report only the marginal guarantee. We did not implement RCPS itself. |
| Angelopoulos, Bates, Candès, Jordan, Lei, "Learn then Test: Calibrating Predictive Algorithms to Achieve Risk Control" (arXiv 2110.01052; listed as Annals of Applied Statistics 2025 in a snippet, not verified) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01052 | Casts risk control as multiple hypothesis testing over a grid of tuning parameters, with finite-sample guarantees (snippet). | A natural route to a joint guarantee over several stages' thresholds at once; we use the simpler union bound over per-stage split-conformal thresholds and have not compared the two. |

## (b) Conformal selection and FDR control in drug discovery

| citation | link | what it does | how ours differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jin and Candès, "Selection by Prediction with Conformal p-values", JMLR 24 (2023) 1-41 (arXiv 2210.01408) | https://jmlr.org/papers/v24/22-1176.html | Builds conformal p-values for 'unobserved outcome exceeds a bar' and applies Benjamini-Hochberg to select candidates with finite-sample FDR control; tested on hiring and drug-discovery data (snippet). | It controls the **false discovery rate** (precision of the shortlist); we control **recall** (loss of truly stable MOFs), which is the opposite error. FDR and recall control are different goals and do not imply each other. We have no FDR guarantee. |
| Jin and Candès, "Model-free selective inference under covariate shift via weighted conformal p-values" (arXiv 2307.09291) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09291 | Extends conformal selection to covariate shift using weighted conformal p-values (snippet). | Relevant to our open problem: group-aware splits mimic a shift to new families, but our guarantee assumes exchangeability of groups, not a shift. |
| "Conformal Selection for Efficient and Accurate Compound Screening in Drug Discovery", ChemRxiv preprint, Nov 2024; authors not verified | https://chemrxiv.org/engage/chemrxiv/article-details/672102dcf9980725cf45d9c3 | Applies conformal selection to compound screening with FDR control (snippet). | Same distinction as above (FDR vs recall); single property rather than a stacked set of filters. Preprint, not peer reviewed as far as I could see. |
| "ACS: An interactive framework for conformal selection", arXiv 2507.15825; authors not verified | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.15825 | Title only seen; I did not see an abstract, so I cannot say what it does beyond interactive conformal selection. | Not assessed. |
| "Multi-Condition Conformal Selection", arXiv 2510.08075 (title as given in a search summary; authors not verified) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08075v2 | Handles conjunctive conditions (e.g. 'medium logP') in conformal selection (snippet). | Closest in spirit to stacking several conditions; ours stacks three **model-based** filters with a union bound on recall, and does not control FDR. |

I did not find or verify a reference for a conformal-selection method that controls recall across several stacked filters; I make no claim that none exists.

## (c) Data leakage and group-aware splits in MOF / materials ML

| citation | link | what it does | how ours differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jablonka, Rosen, Krishnapriyan, Smit, "An Ecosystem for Digital Reticular Chemistry", ACS Central Science (2023) (software: mofdscribe) | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10141625/ | Software ecosystem whose splitters group MOFs (e.g. by scaffold hash) to reduce train/test leakage; its documentation has a section on leakage in reticular chemistry (snippet; Chemistry World says the same structure can appear over 1000 times in a dataset, not verified). | Gives MOFid/hash-based groups, which we could **not** compute (released data has no MOFid or CIFs). | DataSAIL, "Data splitting against information leakage" (addendum in PMC12905371; main-paper citation not verified) | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12905371/ | Splits biomolecular data to bound similarity between splits and compares splitters by quantified leakage (snippet). | Not MOF-specific; a similarity-bounded split is the kind of stronger check we have not done. |
